NUST Life Hub / NUST SOCIAL Platform – Full Detailed Idea
1. Core Vision

You are building a social & marketplace platform for university students where:

Students can interact socially by posting content (confessions, rumors, announcements), with optional anonymity.
Societies can post events, and sell tickets for their events.
Students can create their own stores, sell products, and buy from others.
Real-time chat for communication between users.
Notifications and analytics for engagement and activity tracking.
Moderation, reports, and audit logs to keep the platform safe.

Think of it as Instagram + Facebook Groups + Eventbrite + Etsy, but tailored for NUST students.

2. Users and Profiles
Users Table: Each student has an account with email, name, encrypted_name, status, verified.
User Profiles: Contain username, avatar, bio, batch_year.
Roles: ADMIN or USER. Admins can moderate content and societies.

Key features:

Students can follow each other.
Students can have anonymous posts, which hide their identity while keeping them trackable internally for moderation.
3. Social Feed
Posts Table: Students can create different types of posts:
CONFESSION
RUMOR
MARKETPLACE_POST (linking products)
SOCIETY_POST (shared by societies)
ANNOUNCEMENT
Post Media: Attach images, videos, or documents to posts.
Tags: Posts can have multiple tags (post_tags) to help categorize content.
Comments & Reactions: Users can comment, like posts, and vote on rumors.
Statistics: Post stats like likes, views, comments, rumor votes are tracked with triggers.

Anonymity Feature:

Posts can be marked is_anonymous = TRUE. The platform shows them without revealing the user’s name.
4. Societies & Events
Societies Table: Each society has name, description, created_by.
Members Table: Each society has members with roles (ADMIN, MEMBER).
Society Posts: Societies can post announcements or updates.
Events: Societies can create events with title, description, location, and event_date.
Tickets: Each event can have multiple ticket types (VIP, Regular, Student) with price, stock, and sold count.
Purchases: Students can buy tickets, quantity is tracked, and total price calculated.

This allows:

Societies to earn money from events.
Students to browse, book, and pay for tickets.
5. Marketplace
Stores Table: Students can create a store with name and description.
Products Table: Products belong to stores, with price, stock, and optional media attachments.
Orders Table: Tracks student purchases.
Order Items Table: Products inside an order, quantity, and price.
Payments Table: Tracks payment methods (COD, Card, EasyPaisa, JazzCash) and status.
Addresses: Students can specify shipping addresses.

Key Points:

Students can sell their own products.
Platform handles purchases, payments, and delivery info.
Marketplace integrates naturally with posts, so products can be shared on the feed.
6. Chat System
Conversations: A chat room between 2+ users.
Participants: Users in a conversation, track last_read_at.
Messages: Messages sent by users with timestamps.
Media: Optional media attachments.

This allows:

Private or group chat.
Students and societies can communicate directly.
7. Notifications
Students are notified for:
New comments or likes on their posts
New followers
Order updates
Event reminders
Society announcements
8. Moderation
Reports Table: Users can report posts, comments, products, or users.
Audit Logs: All critical actions (post deletion, ticket sale, user ban) are logged.
Admins review reports and resolve issues.
9. Extra Features
Post Scoring & Feed Cache: Feed for each student is scored (e.g., likes, views, interactions) and cached for fast retrieval.
Rumor Voting System: True/False voting for rumors to track credibility.
Triggers: Automatic updating of statistics like likes, comments, votes.
10. How It All Connects
Users → Can post, comment, react, follow.
Societies → Can post, create events, sell tickets.
Events → Linked to societies → tickets sold → purchases by students.
Stores & Products → Linked to users → orders → payments.
Posts → Can be anonymous → reactions and comments tracked → statistics updated automatically.
Chat & Notifications → Keeps users engaged.
11. Student Flow Example
Alice registers → creates profile.
Bob posts a rumor anonymously.
Alice reacts, comments.
CS Society posts a new event → VIP & regular tickets.
Alice buys 2 VIP tickets.
Alice sets up a store → sells NUST merch.
Bob buys a t-shirt → order & payment logged.
Both chat about the event → receive notifications.

✅ This setup allows the platform to be a complete social + marketplace + events hub for university students.
