return (\n    <AuthLayout\n      gradient=\"purple-pink\"\n      icon=\"🔑\"\n      title=\"Reset Password\"\n      subtitle=\"Enter your email to receive a reset link\"\n    >\n      {sent && (\n        <Alert \n          type=\"success\" \n          message=\"Check your email for reset instructions!\"\n        />\n      )}\n      \n      <form onSubmit={handleSubmit} className=\"space-y-6 mt-6\">\n        <Input\n          type=\"email\"\n          icon=\"email\"\n          value={email}\n          onChange={(e) => setEmail(e.target.value)}\n          placeholder=\"your@email.com\"\n          required\n        />\n        \n        <Button\n          gradient=\"purple-pink\"\n          fullWidth\n          isLoading={loading}\n          type=\"submit\"\n        >\n          Send Reset Link\n        </Button>\n      </form>\n    </AuthLayout>\n  );\n}\n```\n\n---\n\n## 🎯 Next Steps\n\n### Immediate Tasks\n1. ✅ **Auth Pages** - Complete\n2. ⏳ **Home Feed Page** - Use teal-cyan etc.)\n2. **Secondary Actions**: Use `bg-white/5 border-white/10` with hover state\n3. **Danger Actions**: Use `bg-red-500/10 border-red-500/30` with red accent text\n4. **Success States**: Use green colors for confirmations\n5. **Error States**: Use red for errors and warnings\n\n### Typography Hierarchy\n1. **Main Title**: `text-3xl font-bold text-white`\n2. **Subtitle**: `text-gray-300 text-sm`\n3. **Form Labels**: `text-sm font-semibold text-gray-200`\n4. **Body Text**: `text-sm text-gray-300`\n5. **Helper Text**: `text-xs text-gray-400`\n\n### Spacing Consistency\n- Form fields: `mb-6` or `space-y-6`\n- Cards: `p-8`\n- Containers: `p-4` on mobile, scales with breakpoints\n- Gaps between elements: `gap-3` for small, `gap-4` for medium\n\n---\n\n## ✨ Special Effects\n\n###
